# MIT 8.012 — Classical Mechanics

This repository contains my complete work for MIT 8.012: Classical Mechanics,
following the structure and philosophy of Kleppner & Kolenkow. The course
develops classical mechanics from first principles, emphasizing physical
reasoning, conservation laws, and stability rather than rote formula use.

The focus throughout is on understanding internal structure, limiting cases,
and the geometric meaning of motion.

---

## Scope of the Course

MIT 8.012 presents classical mechanics as a coherent framework for analyzing
motion. Topics covered include:

- Newton’s laws and inertial frames
- Momentum, work, and energy
- Conservation laws and symmetry principles
- One- and two-dimensional motion
- Rotating reference frames and fictitious forces
- Centripetal motion and stability
- Rigid-body dynamics
- Angular momentum and torque
- Gyroscopic motion and precession
- Central-force motion
- Effective potentials and equilibrium analysis
- Stability and small oscillations
- Non-inertial frames and rotating systems

Geometry, energy methods, and angular momentum are used as unifying tools
throughout.

## Problem-Solving Approach

A consistent methodology was followed across all problems:

- Identify the relevant physical regime and reference frame
- Determine conserved quantities before writing equations
- Use symmetry and geometry to simplify the analysis
- Apply energy and angular momentum methods where appropriate
- Analyze stability and limiting behavior explicitly
- Interpret results in terms of physical motion, not just algebra

The emphasis is on conceptual clarity and structural understanding.
